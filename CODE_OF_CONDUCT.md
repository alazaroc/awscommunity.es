# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make participation in AWS Community Spain a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, kind and constructive.
- Accept well-reasoned criticism gracefully, and give it the same way.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Assume good faith and ask before assuming.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who violates it.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to [Alejandro Lázaro Chueca](https://github.com/alazaroc) through a [private GitHub advisory](https://github.com/alazaroc/awscommunity.es/security/advisories/new). All reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly and confidentially.
